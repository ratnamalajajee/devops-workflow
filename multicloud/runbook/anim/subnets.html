  1. AWSEach subnet lives in one AZ So every tier gets two. The load balancer uses snet-public-a and snet-public-b.
  2. AWSPods share the node subnets EKS nodes and pods both take IPs from snet-private-a and snet-private-b.
  3. AWSA DB subnet group spans both AZs dbsg-shopnow-dev holds snet-db-a and snet-db-b for RDS and Aurora (steps 19-20).
  4. AzureApp Gateway needs a subnet of its own Only App Gateways go in snet-appgw 10.10.16.0/24 (step 21).
  5. AzureOne subnet covers every zone AKS nodes take IPs from snet-aks. Pods use the overlay 192.168.0.0/16 instead.
  6. AzureSome subnets are handed to one service snet-db is delegated to PostgreSQL flexible server, snet-func to Functions.
  7. Google CloudRegional subnets cover every zone GKE nodes take IPs from snet-gke 10.20.0.0/20. Load balancers need no subnet.
  8. Google CloudPods and services use secondary ranges snet-gke carries extra ranges: pods 10.24.0.0/14 and services 10.28.0.0/20.
  9. Google CloudDatabases use Private Services Access Cloud SQL, AlloyDB and Filestore get IPs from 10.30.0.0/16 (steps 17, 19, 20).
