  1. AWSThe app connects to a private endpoint rds-shopnow-dev: PostgreSQL 16 in snet-db-a and -b, with no public access.
  2. AWSA standby in AZ b copies every write That is Multi-AZ. Automated backups are kept for 14 days.
  3. AWSIf AZ a fails, the standby takes over RDS points the same endpoint name at the standby. The app reconnects.
  4. AzureThe app connects inside the VNet psql-shopnow-dev-7k2q sits in its own subnet, snet-db. Private DNS finds it.
  5. AzureA standby in another zone copies writes That is zone-redundant high availability. Backups are kept for 14 days.
  6. AzureIf its zone fails, the standby takes over The server name stays the same, so the app just reconnects.
  7. Google CloudThe app connects to a private IP sql-shopnow-dev takes its IP from the step 06 range. Public IP is off.
  8. Google CloudA standby in another zone copies writes A regional (HA) instance. Daily backups at 02:00, plus point-in-time recovery.
  9. Google CloudIf its zone fails, the standby takes over Cloud SQL moves the same private IP to the standby. The app reconnects.
